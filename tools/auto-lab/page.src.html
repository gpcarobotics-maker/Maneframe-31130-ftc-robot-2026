Pedro coordinates

Inches. (0, 0) is the bottom-left corner: red alliance wall on the left, audience at the bottom, so the field runs to (144, 144). Heading 0° faces right (toward the blue wall) and turns counter-clockwise, as in Pedro 3. Set your robot's start pose with the same numbers and the paths line up.

The physics, field, HIVEs, balls and auto aim are copied from the BioBuzz Test Drive sim, including its guesses. The follower here mimics Pedro's behaviour and is not Pedro itself, so tune on the real robot.
