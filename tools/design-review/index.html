    Turntable fit

    The real AndyMark Robits 5.5 in. ID turntable (am-5470) dropped onto the robot, seen from above with the front at the top. Its heights match the CAD: plate top at 130 mm, bearing to 140 mm, gear to 153.4 mm, and the turret base sits at 153 mm. What changes is the gear's size and where the drive goes.

    Turret drive optionTurret speedFits the free pocket?Notes

    Two small parts you'll need either way. The kit's 50-tooth pinion has a 3/8 in. hex bore and REV shafts are 5 mm hex, so add AndyMark's 5 mm to 3/8 in. hex adapter (am-4957). The kit's side mount (am-5012) is drilled for AndyMark NeveRest motors: check its holes against your motor, and CNC a small adapter plate if they don't line up.

    Ideas to fit both balls

    Rule R105 lets the robot grow after the match starts, as long as it stays inside 18 × 24 × 29 in. That's 6 in. (152 mm) longer in one direction and 11 in. taller. All 8 motor ports are used, so anything that moves needs a servo (6 of 8 are free) or a spring.
